Quick start

A first photo in your own scene in about five minutes. No code.

1. Add the camera #

Open Tools > Photoworthy > Photo Studio. On the Start tab press Add Photo Camera.

A Photo Camera appears in the scene, pointed at your game's main camera. Your camera is only borrowed: while a photo screen is open the kit zooms and moves it, and when the screen closes everything is put back exactly as it was.

2. Pick how it plays #

Still on the Start tab, choose a Starting point (Wildlife Discovery is a good first one) and press Create Profile. A Photo Profile, a scoring asset and a catalog are made in Assets/RareGeometryData/Photoworthy and given to the camera. Every setting can be changed afterwards; nothing is locked.

3. Make something photographable #

Select one or more objects in the Hierarchy (an animal, a statue, a door, a character) and press Make Selected Photographable, on the Start tab or in Tools > Photoworthy.

Each object gets a Photo Subject component and a small subject asset named after it ("SM_Red_Fox_01" becomes "Red Fox"). Objects that share a subject asset count as the same kind of thing.

4. Press Play #

Key What it does
C Raise and put away the handheld camera
Mouse wheel Zoom
Space or left mouse button Take the photo
Tab Album
J Journal and requests
P Photo mode
O Options
Esc Back

With a gamepad (Input System installed): D-pad up raises the camera, RT takes the photo, LB and RB zoom, D-pad left and right open the album and journal, D-pad down opens the photo mode.

Raise the camera, point it at your subject and take a photo. The result card shows the photo, what it shows, its score and why.

5. Add a photo request (optional) #

Select a photographable object, open the Requests tab and press one of the buttons: Photograph It, A Good Shot, Doing Something, At A Place or Two Together. The request is added to the catalog and shows in the journal's Requests tab while playing. Take a photo that meets it and it completes.

Something not working? #

Open Tools > Photoworthy > Check Scene For Problems. It lists what is missing or contradictory in plain words, most with a one-click fix. To see exactly why a view would score what it does, open Tools > Photoworthy > Score Inspector and press Judge Game Camera or Judge Scene View. It works before you press Play.

Want to see everything working first? #

Tools > Photoworthy > Open Demo > Meadow Trail is a small wildlife trail with animals, landmarks, six requests and a journal. Creator Lab has one station for each idea. See Demo scenes.
