Support

Need help? Every message gets an answer.

In Unity, Tools > Photoworthy > Help and Support lists every support address (Discord, bug reports, email) with Copy buttons, and Copy system info copies the Photoworthy version, Unity version, render pipeline and platform. Paste it in your message.

  • Discord: https://discord.gg/AmpBDxRAKu (ask in #help)
  • Report a bug: https://github.com/raregeometry/Support/issues
  • Email: raregeometrysupport@gmail.com
  • Website: https://raregeometry.github.io/photoworthy/

Enjoying Photoworthy? A short review on the Asset Store helps a lot.
