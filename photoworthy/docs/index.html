Photoworthy documentation

A photo mode and photography gameplay in one kit: the camera knows what is in every photo, scores it with reasons players can read, and photos complete quests, fill a journal and an album.

  • Unity 6000.0.85f1+
  • Built-in, URP & HDRP
  • No dependencies
  • Full C# source
  • Version 1.0.0

Coming soon to the Asset Store.

Get started

Quick start: five minutes

  1. Import the package. Photoworthy lands in Assets/RareGeometry/Photoworthy/.
  2. Open Tools > Photoworthy > Photo Studio, press Add Photo Camera and pick a starting point.
  3. Select any objects and press Make Selected Photographable.
  4. Press Play: C raises the camera, V switches the camera body, Space takes the photo, Tab opens the album.
